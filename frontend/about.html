About Us

The Sports Portal is a digital platform designed to bring athletes, coaches, and sports administrators together. Our mission is to simplify sports event management, enhance communication, and provide a central hub for sports updates, athlete tracking, and performance analysis.

This Final Year Project is built to support multiple users including Admin, Coaches, and Athletes, ensuring smooth coordination of sports activities, events, and performance records.
